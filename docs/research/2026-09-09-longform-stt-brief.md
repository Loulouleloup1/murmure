# Long-form transcription on Mac: engine choice, VAD/chunking, ANE contention (2026)

## Top 5 actionable points
1. **Do not run long files through the live-dictation WhisperKit path unmodified.** Use `ChunkingStrategy: .vad` with a second, separate model context/queue so the ANE-resident dictation model is never displaced — WhisperKit's own VAD chunking splits on silence boundaries and matches a full-file run in quality ([Forasoft, 2026](https://www.forasoft.com/blog/article/speech-recognition-with-neural-networks-on-ios-1621)).
2. **Prefer Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 (FluidAudio CoreML) for long-form batch work** on a 16 GB Mac: ~110× RTF on M4 Pro (1 min audio ≈ 0.5 s), runs on ANE/CPU, covers French among 25 European languages ([FluidInference model card, 2026](https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3-coreml); [MacParakeet, 2026](https://macparakeet.com/blog/fluidaudio-speech-ai-sdk/)). This is materially faster than any Whisper variant and keeps the same Swift/CoreML integration story as WhisperKit.
3. **large-v3-turbo/distil-large-v3 remain the fallback for quality-critical French passages** — 4-6× speed of large-v3 at near-equal quality, 9-22× RT on M3 depending on Metal vs ANE dispatch ([JustVoice, 2026](https://justvoice.ai/blog/whisper-benchmark-apple-silicon-m3-m4); [WhisperNotes, 2026](https://whispernotes.app/blog/introducing-whisper-large-v3-turbo)).
4. **AVFoundation cannot decode MKV/WebM-Opus/Vorbis** — Core Audio has no native decoder for these codecs; mp4/mov (AAC/H.264/HEVC) are fine via `AVAssetReader` → `AVAssetReaderTrackOutput` with `kAudioFormatLinearPCM` settings ([macios wiki / Codeberg mkvtoolnix issue, 2026](https://codeberg.org/mbunkus/mkvtoolnix/issues/6209)). A silent conversion fallback (bundled ffmpeg or user-facing "unsupported container" message) is required for YouTube-downloaded webm/mkv.
5. **Long-form Whisper hallucination/repetition loops are a known, unresolved class of bug**, worse after silence gaps, sometimes running for many minutes of a long file; VAD pre-segmentation (cut on silence before decoding) is the community-accepted mitigation, not a decoding-side fix ([whisper.cpp issue #3744, 2026](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp/issues/3744); [whisper.cpp discussion #2286](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp/discussions/2286)).

## Brief

**WhisperKit long-audio API.** `WhisperKit.transcribe(audioPath:)` accepts arbitrary-length audio; long-form support (e.g. a 90-minute lecture "in one go") is an explicit selling point, and chunking is tunable via `ChunkingStrategy: .vad` (split at silence) or `.none`, with an `.incremental(chunkDuration:chunkBufferSize:)` variant also referenced ([Forasoft, 2026](https://www.forasoft.com/blog/article/speech-recognition-with-neural-networks-on-ios-1621)). Direct confirmation of `EnergyVAD`, `concurrentWorkerCount`, and the exact `TranscriptionCallback` signature was not found in the sources retrieved this round — treat as a gap (see below); the argmax-oss-swift repo is the canonical source ([GitHub argmaxinc/argmax-oss-swift](https://github.com/argmaxinc/argmax-oss-swift)).

**Throughput.** On M3, large-v3-turbo reaches 9.1× RT via whisper.cpp+Metal and 22× RT via CoreML+ANE dispatch; WhisperKit's own KV-cache optimisation cut decoder-forward latency 8.4→4.6 ms on M3 ANE ([JustVoice; arXiv WhisperKit paper, 2507.10860, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.10860v1)). Plain large-v3 sits at 2-3× RT on M3/M4 with Metal. On iPhone 15 Pro, large-v3-turbo transcribes 10 min of audio in ~82 s (>7× RT) ([Forasoft, 2026]). Scaling tracks GPU-core count, not CPU count ([voicci.com, 2026](https://voicci.com/blog/apple-silicon-whisper-performance.html)). Memory: large-v3 ≈10 GB unquantised; INT8/4-bit brings it to 1.5-4 GB ([codersera, 2026](https://codersera.com/blog/whisper-large-local-setup-guide-2026/)) — comfortable headroom on 16 GB alongside a resident dictation model, but not alongside a resident 4B LLM too (see Gaps).

**Alternatives.** Parakeet TDT 0.6B v2/v3 via FluidAudio (CoreML, ANE/CPU) or `parakeet-mlx`/`swift-parakeet-mlx` (MLX/GPU) is the standout for long-form batch: v3 does ~110× RT on M4 Pro, multilingual (25 European languages incl. French) ([FluidInference, HuggingFace, 2026](https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3-coreml); [GitHub FluidInference/swift-parakeet-mlx](https://github.com/FluidInference/swift-parakeet-mlx)). `lightning-whisper-mlx` claims 10× over whisper.cpp / 4× over vanilla mlx-whisper, but runs on GPU not ANE, and one MLX-only implementation reportedly draws excess power vs a CoreML encoder ([GitHub mustafaaljadery/lightning-whisper-mlx](https://github.com/mustafaaljadery/lightning-whisper-mlx); [GitHub altalt-org/Lightning-SimulWhisper](https://github.com/altalt-org/Lightning-SimulWhisper)). whisper.cpp+CoreML encoder gives >3× speed-up over CPU-only ([ggml-org/whisper.cpp README](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp)). 2026-era alternatives: Kyutai STT ships a 1B EN/FR model with built-in semantic VAD and ~0.5 s turn latency (strong French, streaming-oriented); Moonshine v2 targets edge/streaming; Canary-Qwen-2.5B and Qwen3-ASR are ranked competitively on WER/latency/licence but Apple-Silicon-native runtimes were not directly confirmed beyond the `crispasr` wrapper library, which bundles native libcrispasr with Metal acceleration for macOS ([KiaDev/MarkTechPost, 2026](https://kiadev.net/news/2026-07-23-open-asr-models-2026); [PyPI crispasr](https://pypi.org/project/crispasr/)). Voxtral (Mistral) local-runtime-on-Mac was not confirmed in this pass — gap.

**Timestamps for "click paragraph to jump".** Both Whisper (word/segment timestamps, native) and Parakeet TDT (segment-level timestamps as part of its output format) provide cheap, sufficiently granular timestamps for this feature without needing diarisation.

**Video/audio extraction.** `AVAssetReader` + `AVAssetReaderTrackOutput` with `kAudioFormatLinearPCM` output settings reads mp4/mov (H.264/HEVC + AAC) natively and can be configured for 16 kHz mono directly in the output settings dictionary. mkv containers and WebM-with-Opus/Vorbis are NOT decodable by AVFoundation/Core Audio on macOS — no native codec ([mkvtoolnix Codeberg issue #6209, 2026](https://codeberg.org/mbunkus/mkvtoolnix/issues/6209); Qt Multimedia darwin-backend note in same thread). YouTube audio downloads that land as webm/opus will need either an ffmpeg pre-conversion step or restricting supported inputs to mp4/mov/m4a.

**Dictation-app contention patterns.** VoiceInk (open-source, GPL v3, ~4,300★) uses whisper.cpp locally and also supports Parakeet models, offering per-app "Power Mode" configs, but the specific ANE-contention/queueing mechanism (second model instance vs serialised access) was not documented in public sources found this round — treat as a gap requiring direct source inspection of `Beingpax/VoiceInk` on GitHub.

## Contradictions

1. **large-v3-turbo real-time factor on M3**: JustVoice cites 9.1× RT (whisper.cpp+Metal) vs 22× RT (CoreML+ANE) for the *same model on the same chip* — a 2.4× spread ([JustVoice, 2026](https://justvoice.ai/blog/whisper-benchmark-apple-silicon-m3-m4)) — while voicci.com and promptquorum frame "2-3× RT for large-v3 on M3/M4" as the general Metal baseline ([voicci.com, 2026]). **Verdict: résolue** — these are not contradictory once the backend is fixed: ANE dispatch via CoreML is the outlier speed path, Metal-only is the 2-3× baseline for the *non-turbo* large-v3, and whisper.cpp+Metal turbo sits at 9×. The apparent conflict is a backend/model-variant conflation across sources, not a factual dispute.
2. **lightning-whisper-mlx speed claims vs power draw**: its own README claims 10× over whisper.cpp, while a related MLX-only Lightning-SimulWhisper fork notes MLX-only draws "way too much power" and recommends a CoreML encoder instead ([lightning-whisper-mlx GitHub]; [Lightning-SimulWhisper GitHub]). **Verdict: ouverte** — these are different projects/forks; no third source (e.g. an independent power-draw benchmark) was found to settle whether the flagship `lightning-whisper-mlx` itself has the same power characteristic. Ask: whoever maintains `mustafaaljadery/lightning-whisper-mlx`, or run an in-house battery/thermal test before adopting it for background long-form jobs.
